# Identity Regime Vocabulary

An identity regime specifies criteria governing identity judgments and the
conditions under which those criteria apply.

## Foundational Questions

- **Individuation**: what establishes that a presentation identifies one whole,
  and when presentations identify the same unit?
- **Co-reference**: when do references concern the same referent?
- **Persistence**: what licenses stages across change as stages of the same
  continuing individual?

Regime constructions must connect these questions explicitly.

## Transformation Behavior

Transformation theory specifies operator effects.
A regime supplies the identity criteria against which those effects are
evaluated.

Continuation may depend on the particular step, its states, and relevant
context or history. An operator name alone need not determine the judgment.

## Classification and Profiles

A classification records applicability and identity-persistence judgments
over a specified transformation domain.

A profile summarizes those judgments. Different profiles do not automatically
establish different induced identity relations; that requires an appropriate
separation result.

## Derivation Status

The existing regime catalog, classification tables, and proposed
correspondences are being reassessed.

Catalog membership, a declared classification, or a difference between table
rows does not by itself justify an identity criterion or establish a necessary
or complete taxonomy.

Lean source and reference declarations record the implemented definitions,
assumptions, and proved results.
