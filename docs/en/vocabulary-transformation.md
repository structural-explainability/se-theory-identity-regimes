# Transformation Vocabulary

Transformation theory defines structural operators,
their taxonomy, and their effect semantics.

Identity Regimes independently defines transformation families
for evaluating changes to presentations relative to a declared identity basis.
These families are specified in the Identity Regimes Lean source and
are distinct from Transformation theory's operators and families.

Relating the two vocabularies requires an explicit,
justified correspondence between operations and their effects.
Such a correspondence may support conditional preservation or breakage theorems,
but it does not independently establish the adequacy of an identity criterion.

Each theory retains responsibility for its own definitions and classifications.
Neither theory determines the other's results by terminology alone.
